Introduction

Welcome to Emmanuel's poetry, this page is a collection of thoughts, moments, and memories, written down before they could slip away. Poetry, for me, is a way of holding onto what matters; a feeling, a face, a place I have called home.

About the Poet

Emmanuel is from the Igbo-speaking part of Nigeria and is now based in Bradford. He is passionate about community, storytelling, and celebrating culture. He loves volunteering and currently volunteers at the Bradford Central Foodbank and Bradford Community Broadcasting.
